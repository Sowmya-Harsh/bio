Hi, I'm Sowmya.

Data scientist · graph nerd · pipeline builder · relentless debugger

Hi! I'm Sowmya, a data scientist and machine learning engineer who likes turning messy data into tools people can actually use. I'm finishing a Master's in Data Science at the University of Luxembourg, focused on statistics, machine learning, and AI systems that work end to end.

Right now I'm doing my thesis at the Luxembourg Centre for Systems Biomedicine, untangling gene and protein identifiers across BridgeDb, HGNC, and other biomedical resources (more than 10 million mappings) and giving them a home in a graph-aware Neo4j schema with trust scores built in. On top of that I built a full-stack Python platform so people can ingest, search, and actually see the graph instead of squinting at a spreadsheet.

Before that, I spent a summer with LIST building an OCR-to-RAG pipeline that turned scanned workbooks into a searchable knowledge base, using GPT-4o Vision to pull structured exercises, tables, and audio prompts off the page, wired to a multi-agent LLM pipeline for explainable retrieval.

And before Luxembourg, I was a Business Solutions Analyst at Brane Enterprises in Hyderabad, where I shipped 15+ end-to-end tools and learned how to move fast without breaking the people who depend on your software.

When I'm not on a deadline, I like building things with graphs and language: multi-agent apps, retrieval systems, knowledge-graph miners, and graph neural networks for biomedical data. I like problems where the structure of the data is half the answer.

Before all of this I studied Civil Engineering, which sounds like a detour, but it's where I fell for mathematics. That love stuck: I still tutor secondary-school students (S3 to S7) in maths, and there's nothing like explaining a proof to a teenager to keep your own fundamentals honest.
