Hi! I'm Sowmya, a data scientist and machine learning engineer who likes turning messy data into tools people can actually use. I'm finishing a Master's in Data Science at the University of Luxembourg, focused on statistics, machine learning, and AI systems that work end to end.

Right now I'm a data science intern at the Luxembourg Centre for Systems Biomedicine, building end-to-end deep-learning pipelines to prepare biological data for foundation models, and adapting methods like Optimal Transport and temporal conditioning to get more out of them. A lot of the care goes into the data itself: strict integrity, no leakage across train and test, because a model is only as trustworthy as the pipeline behind it.

Before that, as a research assistant at the University of Luxembourg, I built BioHop, a full-stack tool that maps biological identifiers at scale (10M+ records, scraped and cleaned with parallel processing) and lets people explore them as a Neo4j knowledge graph instead of squinting at a spreadsheet.

I also spent a summer with LIST building an OCR-to-RAG pipeline that turned scanned workbooks into a searchable knowledge base, using GPT-4o Vision to pull structured exercises, tables, and audio prompts off the page, wired to a multi-agent LLM pipeline for explainable retrieval.

And before Luxembourg, I was a Business Solutions Analyst at Brane Enterprises in Hyderabad, where I shipped 15+ end-to-end tools and learned how to move fast without breaking the people who depend on your software.

When I'm not on a deadline, I like building things with data and language: predictive models, multi-agent apps, retrieval systems, and knowledge-graph miners. I like problems where the structure of the data is half the answer.

Before all of this I studied Civil Engineering, which sounds like a detour, but it's where I fell for mathematics. That love stuck: I still tutor secondary-school students (S3 to S7) in maths, and there's nothing like explaining a proof to a teenager to keep your own fundamentals honest.
